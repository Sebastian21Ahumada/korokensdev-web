Privacy policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

The short version: we keep the minimum needed for the game to work, and none of it is sold.

Your name

The one you choose when you start. The people you play with see it, and it's what tells you apart on the scoreboard. You can change it whenever you want.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, we store an identifier your phone provides so we can tell you when it's your turn. It can't identify you outside the game. Turning notifications off in Settings deletes that identifier from the server.

Your matches

Countries, turns, answers and results, for as long as the match exists. When it ends and you close the result, all of it is deleted. A match nobody touches again is deleted on its own after 30 days.

Device settings

Your language and time zone, so questions show up in your language and turns are counted in your time. Volume and sound settings are stored only on your phone and never leave it.

Who it's shared with

Only the services needed to run the game: Firebase (Google) for anonymous sign-in, and Expo's push service for notifications. It is not sold or handed to anyone else.

Children

The game is not aimed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data or for all of it to be deleted, by writing to korokensdev@gmail.com.
