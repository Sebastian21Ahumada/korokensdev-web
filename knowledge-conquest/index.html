How to play

Six mechanics, one idea

Get it right and you gain ground. Get it wrong and it can cost you.

Land conquest

Answer correctly and the neighbouring country turns your colour. A miss breaks the streak.

Boosts

Conquering sometimes drops a reward: extra time, an extra life, a turn back.

Sea routes

The sea can be crossed too. Your empire doesn't stop at the coast.

Battle

A rival's country is fought over with questions: whoever answers better keeps it.

Defence

If you're attacked, you answer to keep what's already yours.

Badges

Thirty achievements to discover, from your first conquest to owning a whole continent.

Three ways to play
