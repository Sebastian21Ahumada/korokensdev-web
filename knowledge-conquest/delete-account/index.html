Delete your account

Last updated: September 17, 2026

You can delete your account and its data from inside the app. If you no longer have the app installed, you can request it here instead.

Playing as a guest

A guest account has no email or password, so signing out of it already can't be undone. From the confirmation you see when you tap "Sign out", deleting the account happens automatically — there is no separate button to look for.

Playing with a linked Google account

Open Profile, scroll to the bottom, and tap "Delete account". Confirm on the warning screen. This is separate from "Sign out", which for a linked account just ends the session and lets you sign back in later — deleting the account is a distinct, deliberate action.

What gets deleted

Your name, your chosen avatar, your stats, badges, purchased avatars, notification tokens, and any offline match result waiting to sync. Your Google sign-in credential is revoked, so it can never be used to access that account again.

What can stay a little longer, and why

If you're in an active match with friends when you delete your account, your countries are released back to neutral immediately — they don't vanish frozen in place, and your friends' match isn't affected. What that match's own history keeps (who attacked whom, who won a country) can't be erased without also erasing your friends' record of a match they're still playing, so it stays until that match itself ends and is closed by everyone in it — the same point at which it's deleted for everyone anyway, whether or not you're still in it. Nothing in it can be used to identify you once your account is anonymized.

Don't have the app anymore?

Email korokensdev@gmail.com from the address you signed in with (or tell us the display name and roughly when you played), and ask for your account to be deleted. It's handled by hand, usually within a few days.
